How escrow works

Your funds sit in a contract, not with us. Neither side can move them alone.

  1. Agree on milestonesClient and freelancer split the job into paid stages.
  2. Client funds escrowUSDC, USDT or ETH is locked in the contract.
  3. Approve to releaseEach approval pays that milestone instantly.
  4. Dispute if neededStaked arbitrators review evidence and rule on-chain.

Questions

Do I need crypto to start?

You can browse and message with an email login. You need a wallet to fund escrow or get paid.

Who can move the escrowed funds?

Only the contract’s rules can: release on client approval, refund on cancel, or a dispute ruling. ValeDeFi never holds your funds.

What happens in a dispute?

Either side uploads evidence to IPFS. Staked arbitrators vote, and the ruling executes after a timelock.

What does ValeDeFi charge?

3% of each released milestone, with discounts for paying in $VALE.
